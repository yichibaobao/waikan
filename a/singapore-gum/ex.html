1a sticky situation棘手的局面、尴尬的处境

1-1 忘了她的名字让我很尴尬。

Forgetting her name put me in a sticky situation.

1-2 他靠一张嘴摆脱了困境。

He talked his way out of a sticky situation.

2city-state城市国家

2-1 新加坡是个小而富裕的城市国家。

Singapore is a small but wealthy city-state.

2-2 古代雅典是一个强大的城邦。

Ancient Athens was a powerful city-state.

3show little sign of几乎没有……的迹象

3-1 雨丝毫没有要停的意思。

The rain showed no sign of stopping.

3-2 价格几乎没有下降的迹象。

Prices show little sign of falling.

4relent让步、松口；（风雨）减弱

4-1 她父母最终松口，让她去了。

Her parents finally relented and let her go.

4-2 暴风雨丝毫没有减弱的迹象。

The storm showed no sign of relenting.

5baffling令人费解的、莫名其妙的

5-1 说明书完全看不懂。

The instructions were completely baffling.

5-2 医生们对她的病情感到困惑。

Doctors were baffled by her illness.

6raise eyebrows引人侧目、令人惊讶

6-1 她那头亮绿色的头发在办公室引人侧目。

Her bright green hair raised eyebrows at the office.

6-2 这个价格让一些人吃了一惊。

The price raised a few eyebrows.

7reasoning推理、理由

7-1 我不明白这条规定背后的道理。

I don't understand the reasoning behind this rule.

7-2 她的理由很简单：太贵了。

Her reasoning was simple: it's too expensive.

8spend a fortune花一大笔钱

8-1 他们在婚礼上花了一大笔钱。

They spent a fortune on the wedding.

8-2 那件外套一定贵得要命。

That coat must have cost a fortune.

9scrape off刮掉

9-1 他把旧漆刮掉了。

He scraped off the old paint.

9-2 我花了十分钟把冰刮掉。

I spent ten minutes scraping off the ice.

10stairwell楼梯井、楼梯间

10-1 孩子们在楼梯间里玩。

The kids played in the stairwell.

10-2 楼梯间又黑又冷。

The stairwell was dark and cold.

11the last straw压垮骆驼的最后一根稻草、忍无可忍

11-1 又迟到，这让人忍无可忍了。

Being late again was the last straw.

11-2 电视坏了，这成了压垮骆驼的最后一根稻草。

When the TV broke, that was the last straw.

12according to most accounts据大多数说法

12-1 大家都说她是个出色的老师。

By all accounts, she is a brilliant teacher.

12-2 据大多数说法，火是从厨房烧起来的。

According to most accounts, the fire started in the kitchen.

13vandal故意破坏公物的人

13-1 有人把公交候车亭砸了。

Vandals smashed the bus shelter.

13-2 警察通过监控拍到了那个破坏公物的人。

The police caught the vandal on camera.

14stall停顿、熄火；拖延

14-1 车在红绿灯前熄火了。

The car stalled at the traffic lights.

14-2 和谈再次陷入僵局。

Peace talks have stalled again.

15pride oneself on以……为荣、为……而自豪

15-1 这家酒店以服务为傲。

The hotel prides itself on its service.

15-2 她以从不迟到为荣。

She prides herself on never being late.

16orderly整齐的、有秩序的

16-1 请有序地离开大楼。

Please leave the building in an orderly way.

16-2 她的桌子总是整整齐齐。

Her desk is always neat and orderly.

17regulate管理、规范、调节

17-1 政府计划规范网络游戏。

The government plans to regulate online games.

17-2 出汗有助于调节体温。

Sweat helps regulate body temperature.

18part of the deal（不得不接受的）一部分条件

18-1 周末加班是这份工作的一部分。

Working weekends is part of the deal.

18-2 去巴黎就得接受人多。

Crowds are part of the deal when you visit Paris.

19nanny state保姆式国家（管得太多的政府）

19-1 有人说新规定是保姆式国家管疯了。

Some people call the new rules the nanny state gone mad.

19-2 他讨厌政府什么都管。

He hates the idea of a nanny state.

20fussy挑剔的、过分讲究的；琐碎的

20-1 我儿子特别挑食。

My son is a very fussy eater.

20-2 我不挑，哪家餐厅都行。

I'm not fussy — any restaurant is fine.

21unbothered不在意的、无所谓的

21-1 她似乎一点也不在意下雨。

She seemed unbothered by the rain.

21-2 他保持冷静，毫不在意。

He stayed calm and unbothered.

22spotless一尘不染的、非常干净的

22-1 酒店房间一尘不染。

The hotel room was spotless.

22-2 他的驾驶记录毫无污点。

He has a spotless driving record.

23persistent持续的、顽固的；坚持不懈的

23-1 我咳嗽了好几周都不好。

I've had a persistent cough for weeks.

23-2 她不停追问，终于得到了答复。

Her persistent questions finally got an answer.

24myth神话；（错误的）说法

24-1 金鱼几秒钟就忘事，这是个误区。

It's a myth that goldfish forget everything in seconds.

24-2 她爱读希腊神话。

She loves reading Greek myths.

25cane (caned)用藤条打（一种体罚）

25-1 过去，学生对老师无礼可能会挨藤条。

In the past, pupils could be caned for being rude to a teacher.

25-2 他因这项罪行被判鞭刑。

He was sentenced to be caned for the crime.

26vandalise (vandalising)故意破坏（公物、他人财物）

26-1 又有人把电话亭搞坏了。

Someone has vandalised the phone box again.

26-2 那几个男孩在破坏校车时被抓了个正着。

The boys were caught vandalising a school bus.

27for one's part就某人而言

27-1 就我而言，我很乐意等。

For my part, I'm happy to wait.

27-2 公司方面则否认一切。

The company, for its part, denies everything.

28indifference漠不关心、冷淡

28-1 他听着，完全无动于衷。

He listened with complete indifference.

28-2 她对这个消息漠不关心。

She was indifferent to the news.

29lose the habit不再有这个习惯

29-1 我已经不再睡前看书了。

I've lost the habit of reading before bed.

29-2 尽量别丢了锻炼的习惯。

Try not to get out of the habit of exercising.

30Either way不管怎样（两种情况都一样）；无论哪种

30-1 坐公交也好，走路也好，反正我们都会迟到。

We can take the bus or walk — either way, we'll be late.

30-2 我不知道他是忘了还是根本不在乎，总之他没打电话。

I don't know if he forgot or just didn't care. Either way, he didn't call.

31chew on嚼；（比喻）仔细琢磨

31-1 那只狗喜欢啃我的鞋。

The dog likes to chew on my shoes.

31-2 给我一天时间琢磨你的提议。

Give me a day to chew on your offer.
