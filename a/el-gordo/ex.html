1cannot afford to承担不起（做某事的后果）；买不起

1-1 我们丢不起这个客户。

We cannot afford to lose this customer.

1-2 我可没钱天天打车。

I can't afford to take a taxi every day.

2skip跳过、不参加

2-1 考试前别不吃早饭。

Don't skip breakfast before an exam.

2-2 第一章可以跳过。

You can skip the first chapter.

3buy into入股、合买；（比喻）相信、认同

3-1 五个朋友合买了同一张彩票。

Five friends bought into the same ticket.

3-2 我不信那些炒作。

I don't buy into all that hype.

4be left out被排除在外、被冷落

4-1 没人喜欢被冷落的感觉。

Nobody likes to feel left out.

4-2 别漏掉任何细节。

Don't leave out any details.

5date back追溯到、始于

5-1 这座城堡可追溯到 12 世纪。

The castle dates back to the 12th century.

5-2 他们的友谊从上学时就开始了。

Their friendship dates back to school.

6broadcast (verb)广播、播出

6-1 比赛会现场直播。

The match will be broadcast live.

6-2 这档节目每周日晚上播出。

The show broadcasts every Sunday night.

7distinctive独特的、与众不同的

7-1 她的笑声很有特点。

She has a very distinctive laugh.

7-2 这栋楼独特的红屋顶很好认。

The building's distinctive red roof is easy to spot.

8chant反复吟唱（的口号、调子）

8-1 人群开始齐声喊他的名字。

The crowd began to chant his name.

8-2 僧侣们唱起缓慢的诵经调。

Monks sang a slow chant.

9fraction一小部分；分数

9-1 价格只是原价的零头。

It costs a fraction of the original price.

9-2 只有少数客人待到很晚。

Only a fraction of the guests stayed late.

10go further更进一步

10-1 有些学校更进一步，完全禁止带手机。

Some schools go further and ban phones completely.

10-2 她更进一步，直接辞了职。

She went further and quit her job.

11split分摊；分开

11-1 我们 AA 吧。

Let's split the bill.

11-2 全班分成了三组。

The class split into three groups.

12club together凑钱、集资（一起买）

12-1 我们凑钱给他买了辆自行车。

We clubbed together to buy him a bike.

12-2 邻居们每年夏天凑钱办一次聚会。

The neighbours club together for a party every summer.

13the heart of……的核心、中心

13-1 信任是任何友谊的核心。

Trust is the heart of any friendship.

13-2 酒店就在老城的中心。

The hotel is in the heart of the old town.

14come up（号码）被抽中；出现、被提到

14-1 谈话中提到了他的名字。

His name came up in the conversation.

14-2 有什么事就给我打电话。

If anything comes up, call me.

15resign辞职

15-1 那位部长被迫辞职。

The minister was forced to resign.

15-2 她辞职去创办自己的公司。

She resigned to start her own company.

16chip in凑份子、出一份钱

16-1 我们大家一起凑钱给她买生日礼物。

We all chipped in for her birthday present.

16-2 你能出几英镑吗？

Can you chip in a few pounds?

17be reminded of被提醒（想起）

17-1 每次闻到玫瑰香，我就想起奶奶。

I'm reminded of my grandmother whenever I smell roses.

17-2 他不想被人提起自己的错误。

He doesn't want to be reminded of his mistake.

18fall on落在……身上；（日期）正好是

18-1 今年我生日正好是星期天。

My birthday falls on a Sunday this year.

18-2 做饭的活落到了我头上。

The job of cooking fell on me.

19household一户人家、家庭

19-1 几乎每户人家都有洗衣机。

Almost every household has a washing machine.

19-2 家庭开支一直在涨。

Household costs keep rising.

20rueful懊悔的、带点遗憾的

20-1 他苦笑了一下。

He gave a rueful smile.

20-2 她回顾自己的选择，自嘲地笑了笑。

She looked back on her choice with a rueful laugh.

21the odds of……的可能性、几率

21-1 那种事发生的可能性微乎其微。

The odds of that happening are tiny.

21-2 他们在不被看好的情况下赢了。

They won against the odds.

22slim（机会）小的；苗条的

22-1 下雨的可能性很小。

There's only a slim chance of rain.

22-2 他们获胜的希望很渺茫。

Their hopes of winning are slim.

23pay out（大笔）支付、赔付

23-1 保险公司很快就赔付了。

The insurance company paid out quickly.

23-2 这个游戏经常发小奖。

The game pays out small prizes often.

24refund退款

24-1 我想退款。

I'd like a refund, please.

24-2 他们全额退了钱。

They refunded the full price.

25grumble抱怨、发牢骚

25-1 他总在抱怨他的老板。

He's always grumbling about his boss.

25-2 乘客们抱怨晚点。

Passengers grumbled about the delay.

26bet (n.)赌注、打赌

26-1 他在那匹马上下了注。

He placed a bet on the horse.

26-2 带把伞总没错。

Taking an umbrella is a safe bet.

27ritual仪式、例行习惯

27-1 下午茶是我们家的固定习惯。

Tea in the afternoon is a family ritual.

27-2 婚礼按照古老的仪式进行。

The wedding followed an ancient ritual.

28No sooner ... than（倒装）一……就……

28-1 我们刚到就下起了雨。

No sooner had we arrived than it started to rain.

28-2 她刚走他就打来了电话。

No sooner had she left than he called.

29spray喷、喷洒

29-1 孩子们互相喷水。

The kids sprayed each other with water.

29-2 农民春天给庄稼喷药。

Farmers spray the crops in spring.

30banner横幅、旗帜

30-1 球迷举起了一条巨大的横幅。

Fans held up a huge banner.

30-2 街上挂着一条横幅宣布节日。

A banner across the street announced the festival.

31boast of / about夸耀、吹嘘

31-1 他喜欢炫耀他的车。

He likes to boast about his car.

31-2 这家酒店以美景自豪。

The hotel boasts of a fine view.

32superstition迷信

32-1 那只是迷信。

It's just a superstition.

32-2 很多球迷都有些小迷信。

Many sports fans have little superstitions.

33play one's part起作用、尽一份力

33-1 运气也起了作用。

Luck played its part, too.

33-2 每个人都要尽一份力。

Everyone must play their part.

34outlet销售点、门店；（情绪的）发泄途径

34-1 这家连锁店在全国有 200 家门店。

The chain has 200 outlets across the country.

34-2 音乐是我释放压力的方式。

Music is my outlet for stress.

35rub擦、搓、蹭

35-1 他揉了揉疲惫的眼睛。

He rubbed his tired eyes.

35-2 把乳霜揉进皮肤里。

Rub the cream into your skin.

36for all尽管（有）

36-1 尽管有种种问题，我还是爱这座城市。

For all its problems, I love this city.

36-2 尽管很有名，她生活得很简朴。

For all her fame, she lives simply.

37jackpot头奖、巨额奖金

37-1 她上周中了头奖。

She won the jackpot last week.

37-2 订到这家酒店，我们真是走大运了。

We really hit the jackpot with this hotel.

38be in it together同舟共济、一起面对

38-1 别担心，我们是一条船上的。

Don't worry, we're all in it together.

38-2 队员们觉得大家是同舟共济的。

The team felt they were in it together.

39half the fun一半的乐趣、乐趣所在

39-1 计划旅行本身就是乐趣的一半。

Planning the trip is half the fun.

39-2 去的路上就是乐趣所在。

Getting there is half the fun.
