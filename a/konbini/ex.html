1hands (workers)人手、干活的人

1-1 收庄稼需要更多人手。

We need more hands for the harvest.

1-2 厨房每到周五就缺人手。

The kitchen is short of hands on Fridays.

2the strain on sth……承受的压力

2-1 热浪给电网造成了巨大压力。

The heatwave put a huge strain on the power grid.

2-2 长时间工作让她吃不消，脸上都能看出疲惫。

Long hours are taking a toll; you can see the strain on her face.

3post (a parcel)邮寄（英式）

3-1 我周一把生日贺卡寄出去了。

I posted the birthday card on Monday.

3-2 你上班路上能帮我寄一下这封信吗？

Could you post this letter on your way to work?

4staff (verb)为……配备人员；在……上班

4-1 服务台由志愿者值守。

The help desk is staffed by volunteers.

4-2 夜里很难给医院排够人手。

It is hard to staff a hospital at night.

5grow scarce变得稀缺

5-1 随着夏天过去，水越来越少。

Water grew scarce as the summer went on.

5-2 市中心便宜的公寓越来越难找。

Cheap flats are growing scarce in the city centre.

6belong to属于

6-1 这把伞是我妹妹的。

This umbrella belongs to my sister.

6-2 这块地以前归教会所有。

The land once belonged to the church.

7restock补货、重新进货

7-1 店员每天晚上补货。

Staff restock the shelves every night.

7-2 暴风雨过后这家店一直没补货。

The shop has not restocked since the storm.

8the till收银台、收银机（英式）

8-1 请到门口的收银台付款。

Please pay at the till by the door.

8-2 收银台前排着长队。

There was a long queue at the till.

9known locally as当地叫作……

9-1 这座山当地人叫“睡巨人”，很好爬。

The hill, known locally as the Sleeping Giant, is easy to climb.

9-2 他开了一家当地人叫“蓝门”的咖啡馆。

He runs a café known locally as the Blue Door.

10one for every N people每 N 个人就有一个

10-1 每五百个居民有一名医生。

There is a doctor for every 500 residents.

10-2 学校每个班都配了一个。

The school has one for every class.

11grow into发展成、长成

11-1 那家小咖啡馆已经发展成有三十家店的连锁品牌。

The small café has grown into a chain of 30 shops.

11-2 这个爱好慢慢变成了一门生意。

The hobby slowly grew into a business.

12public utility公用事业（水电燃气等）

12-1 在大多数国家，供水被当作公用事业。

Water is treated as a public utility in most countries.

12-2 对很多人来说，网络如今就是一种公用事业。

For many people, the internet is now a public utility.

13collect (a parcel)领取（包裹等）

13-1 五点以后可以到店里取你订的东西。

You can collect your order from the shop after five.

13-2 我去学校接了孩子。

I collected the kids from school.

14withdraw cash取现金

14-1 出发前我得取点现金。

I need to withdraw cash before the trip.

14-2 他从取款机取了两百英镑。

He withdrew £200 from the machine.

15count on指望、依靠

15-1 你可以放心，她一定准时。

You can count on her to be on time.

15-2 我们本来指望野餐那天天气好。

We were counting on good weather for the picnic.

16rest on建立在……上、取决于

16-1 整个计划都建立在一个大胆的猜测上。

The whole plan rests on one big guess.

16-2 她的说法全靠一个证人。

Her case rested on a single witness.

17room for error出错的余地

17-1 只剩十分钟了，一点也不能出错。

With only ten minutes left, there is no room for error.

17-2 飞行员要经过训练，因为几乎容不得出错。

Pilots are trained because there is little room for error.

18franchise owner加盟店主

18-1 大多数加盟店主都在自己店里干活。

Most franchise owners work in their own shops.

18-2 当了二十年经理之后，她自己开了一家加盟店。

She became a franchise owner after 20 years as a manager.

19a hefty share of相当大的一部分

19-1 房租占了我工资的一大块。

Rent takes a hefty share of my salary.

19-2 他因为超速交了一大笔罚款。

He paid a hefty fine for speeding.

20in return for作为……的交换

20-1 她帮邻居遛狗，换免费的午饭吃。

She walks the neighbour's dog in return for free lunches.

20-2 他们帮忙修房子，换来了免交房租。

They got free rent in return for fixing the house.

21go to waste被浪费掉

21-1 别让这么多吃的浪费掉。

Don't let all that food go to waste.

21-2 上周一半的菜都浪费了。

Half the vegetables went to waste last week.

22sit empty空着、闲置

22-1 那套公寓空置了两年。

The flat sat empty for two years.

22-2 如今城里有成千上万间办公室空着。

Thousands of offices now sit empty in the city.

23famously出了名地

23-1 这里的火车准点是出了名的。

The trains are famously punctual.

23-2 他记不住人名是出了名的。

He was famously bad at remembering names.

24around the clock昼夜不停、二十四小时

24-1 这个病房的护士全天轮班。

Nurses work around the clock in this ward.

24-2 团队连轴转修这个漏洞。

The team worked round the clock to fix the bug.

25increasingly越来越

25-1 城里的公寓越来越贵。

Flats in the city are increasingly expensive.

25-2 人们越来越多地在网上购物。

People increasingly shop online.

26working-age population劳动年龄人口

26-1 这个国家的劳动年龄人口在减少。

The country's working-age population is falling.

26-2 劳动年龄人口减少，意味着纳税人变少。

A shrinking working-age population means fewer taxpayers.

27go unfilled（职位）一直空着、招不到人

27-1 每年都有很多教师岗位招不到人。

Many teaching jobs go unfilled every year.

27-2 这个职位空了六个月。

The post went unfilled for six months.

28night shift夜班

28-1 她在医院上夜班。

She works the night shift at the hospital.

28-2 夜班工资稍高一点。

Night shifts are paid a little more.

29cover the gaps填补空缺

29-1 员工生病请假时，由志愿者顶上。

Volunteers cover the gaps when staff are off sick.

29-2 我们招了临时工来填补暑期的空缺。

We hired temps to cover the gap over the summer.

30for weeks on end一连好几周

30-1 雨一连下了好几周。

It rained for weeks on end.

30-2 他一连几个小时打游戏。

He played video games for hours on end.

31talking point话题、热议的事

31-1 新体育场成了城里热议的话题。

The new stadium became a big talking point in town.

31-2 她的裙子是当晚大家谈论最多的话题。

Her dress was the main talking point of the evening.

32make up构成、占（比例）

32-1 观众大部分是学生。

Students make up most of the audience.

32-2 房租占我开支的三分之一。

Rent makes up a third of my spending.

33experiment with尝试、试着用

33-1 那家咖啡馆试过周日营业。

The café experimented with opening on Sundays.

33-2 厨师们喜欢尝试新口味。

Chefs love to experiment with new flavours.

34chip away at一点点削弱

34-1 一个个小谎言会慢慢消磨信任。

Small lies chip away at trust.

34-2 物价上涨正一点点吃掉人们的积蓄。

Rising prices are chipping away at people's savings.

35indispensable不可或缺的

35-1 走这条徒步路线，一张好地图必不可少。

A good map is indispensable on this hike.

35-2 她让自己在团队里不可或缺。

She made herself indispensable to the team.

36in the first place当初、原本

36-1 你当初为什么要答应？

Why did you agree to it in the first place?

36-2 当初让小镇安全的那些规定，现在正在被取消。

The rules that made the town safe in the first place are now being dropped.

37ageing老龄化的

37-1 欧洲人口正在老龄化。

Europe has an ageing population.

37-2 那座老旧的桥需要维修。

The ageing bridge needs repairs.

38take sth for granted把……视为理所当然

38-1 我们理所当然地认为灯会亮。

We take for granted that the lights will come on.

38-2 干净的水很容易被当作理所当然。

Clean water is easy to take for granted.

39prop up支撑、撑住

39-1 那堵旧墙用木梁撑着。

The old wall is propped up with wooden beams.

39-2 政府花了几十亿救助那家航空公司。

The government spent billions to prop up the airline.

40shortfall短缺、缺口

40-1 这所学校缺十名老师。

The school faces a shortfall of ten teachers.

40-2 为了填补预算缺口，税涨了。

Taxes rose to cover the budget shortfall.

41show up显现、露出来

41-1 损坏只有在强光下才看得出来。

The damage only shows up under bright light.

41-2 压力常常会反映在睡眠上。

Stress often shows up in your sleep.

42sooner or later迟早

42-1 你迟早得告诉她。

Sooner or later, you will have to tell her.

42-2 每个人迟早都会犯这个错。

Everyone makes that mistake sooner or later.

43among the first to do sth最早做某事的那一批之一

43-1 她是最早到聚会的几个人之一。

She was among the first to arrive at the party.

43-2 我们镇是最早通上高速网络的地方之一。

Our town was among the first to get fast internet.
