It was a bold decision to quit her job.

The castle walls were the town's main defence.

She unwraps a sandwich at her desk every day at noon.

Watch the eagle swoop down towards the river.

The dog made off with a sausage from the barbecue.

8shriek尖叫

8-1 冰冷的浪打过来，孩子们尖叫起来。

The kids shrieked when the cold wave hit them.

8-2 她听到笑话笑得尖叫。

She shrieked with laughter at the joke.

9retreat撤退、退到（安全处）

9-1 客人一来，猫就躲到床底下去了。

The cat retreated under the bed when the guests arrived.

9-2 午饭后他总是躲进书房看书。

After lunch he retreats to his study to read.

10play out（事情）发生、上演

10-1 每个星期天，我们家都会上演同样的争吵。

The same argument plays out in our house every Sunday.

10-2 我们等着看事情怎么发展吧。

Let's wait and see how things play out.

11cheeky厚脸皮的、调皮放肆的

11-1 别对老师没大没小的。

Don't be cheeky to your teacher.

11-2 他冲我调皮一笑，拿走了最后一块饼干。

He gave me a cheeky grin and took the last biscuit.

12scavenge捡食、翻找（剩余物）

12-1 狐狸夜里在垃圾桶里翻找残羹剩饭。

Foxes scavenge for scraps in the bins at night.

12-2 我们在海滩上捡木头生火。

We scavenged wood from the beach for the fire.

She picked through the box of old photos.

13-2 几只鸟在垃圾里翻找。

Birds were picking through the rubbish.

Our savings dwindled during the long holiday.

The crowd dwindled as the rain got heavier.

The room soon filled up with smoke.

Her eyes filled up with tears.

The storm moved inland overnight.

They live in a small inland village.

17adapt适应

Children adapt quickly to a new school.

17-2 公司转向了网上销售。

The company adapted to online sales.

18easy to come by容易弄到

18-1 在伦敦很难找到便宜的公寓。

Cheap flats are hard to come by in London.

18-2 夏天新鲜水果很容易买到。

In summer, fresh fruit is easy to come by.

19get away with做了（坏事）而没受惩罚

19-1 他作弊了，却什么事也没有。

He cheated and got away with it.

19-2 你不能天天迟到还不受处罚。

You can't get away with being late every day.

20at one's wits' end束手无策、急得没办法

20-1 一周没睡好，这对新手父母已经束手无策了。

The new parents were at their wits' end after a week without sleep.

20-2 这台打印机真让我没辙了。

I'm at my wits' end with this printer.

21everything from A to B从 A 到 B 什么都……

21-1 这个市场什么都卖，从鱼到手机壳。

The market sells everything from fish to phone cases.

21-2 我们什么都聊，从足球到哲学。

We talked about everything from football to philosophy.

22fine (noun)罚款

22-1 她在医院外面吃了一张停车罚单。

She got a parking fine outside the hospital.

22-2 司机开车用手机会被罚款。

Drivers are fined for using their phones.

23scare off把……吓跑

23-1 狗一叫，把小偷吓跑了。

The dog barked and scared the burglars off.

23-2 高价可能会把游客吓跑。

High prices may scare off tourists.

24catch on明白过来；流行起来

24-1 我过了一会儿才明白这个笑话。

It took me a while to catch on to the joke.

24-2 这个新应用一直没真正流行起来。

The new app never really caught on.

25come up with想出（主意、办法）

25-1 她给店想了个巧妙的名字。

She came up with a clever name for the shop.

25-2 我们得在周五前想出个计划。

We need to come up with a plan by Friday.

26stare sb down瞪到对方退缩

26-1 猫一直瞪着狗，直到狗退缩。

The cat stared the dog down.

26-2 别跟一头发怒的公牛对视。

Don't try to stare down an angry bull.

27approach (verb)靠近、接近

27-1 街上有个陌生人朝我走过来。

A stranger approached me in the street.

27-2 冬天很快就要到了。

Winter is approaching fast.

28hesitate犹豫

28-1 她犹豫了一下才拆开信。

She hesitated before opening the letter.

28-2 需要帮忙就尽管给我打电话。

Don't hesitate to call me if you need help.

29give up放弃

29-1 他试了三次就放弃了。

He gave up after three tries.

29-2 永远不要放弃你的梦想。

Never give up on your dreams.

30altogether完全、彻底

30-1 噪音完全停了。

The noise stopped altogether.

30-2 我们可能会彻底取消这次旅行。

We might cancel the trip altogether.

31be wary of对……有戒心、提防

31-1 猫对陌生人有戒心。

Cats are wary of strangers.

31-2 好得不像真的优惠，要多留个心眼。

Be wary of offers that sound too good to be true.

32fight back还击、反抗

32-1 那个恶霸推他时，他还手了。

When the bully pushed him, he fought back.

32-2 她演讲时强忍着眼泪。

She fought back tears during the speech.

33there is a catch有个隐藏的问题、有个“但是”

33-1 机票很便宜，但有个问题：不含行李。

The flight is very cheap, but there is a catch: no luggage.

33-2 一年免费披萨？有什么猫腻？

Free pizza for a year? What's the catch?

34a minority of……中的少数

34-1 只有少数学生走路上学。

Only a minority of students walk to school.

34-2 大多数事故是少数司机造成的。

A minority of drivers cause most accidents.

35dare (to) do sth敢于做某事

35-1 没人敢告诉老板。

Nobody dared to tell the boss.

35-2 我不敢从桥上往下看。

I didn't dare look down from the bridge.

36barely几乎没有、勉强

36-1 音乐太吵，我几乎听不见他说话。

I could barely hear him over the music.

36-2 我们差一点就没按时赶到机场。

We barely made it to the airport on time.

37keep an eye on看着、留意

37-1 能帮我看一下包吗？

Can you keep an eye on my bag for a minute?

37-2 老师一直留意着后排那个安静的男孩。

The teacher kept an eye on the quiet boy at the back.

38keep track of掌握……的情况、记着

38-1 我用一个应用记录开支。

I use an app to keep track of my spending.

38-2 我们忘了时间，错过了公交车。

We lost track of time and missed the bus.

39hint at暗示、透露出

39-1 他暗示下周会有重大消息。

He hinted at a big announcement next week.

39-2 乌云预示着暴风雨。

The dark clouds hint at a storm.

40opportunist投机者、善于抓机会的人

40-1 狐狸是聪明的机会主义者，几乎什么都吃。

Foxes are clever opportunists that eat almost anything.

40-2 有些政客完全是投机分子。

Some politicians are pure opportunists.

41lie around被随手乱放

41-1 别把钥匙到处乱放。

Don't leave your keys lying around.

41-2 到处都乱扔着玩具。

There were toys lying around everywhere.

42in decline在衰退、在减少

42-1 那个老行业已经衰落多年了。

The old industry has been in decline for years.

42-2 许多国家的蜜蜂数量在减少。

Bee numbers are in decline in many countries.

43even as正当……的时候（表对比）

43-1 城市繁荣起来的同时，小镇却越来越冷清。

The town grew quieter even as the city boomed.

43-2 就在输掉比赛的时候，他还一直在笑。

He kept smiling even as he lost the game.

44thrive兴旺、长得好

44-1 番茄在暖和的天气里长得好。

Tomatoes thrive in warm weather.

44-2 有些人越有压力越出色。

Some people thrive under pressure.

45hard to justify很难说得过去、很难找到理由

45-1 这么高的价格很难说得过去。

Such a high price is hard to justify.

45-2 你怎么解释花这么多钱是合理的？

How can you justify spending so much?

46when all else fails其他办法都不管用时

46-1 实在不行，就关了再开一次。

When all else fails, turn it off and on again.

46-2 要是其他办法都不行，我们就打车。

If all else fails, we can take a taxi.

47look sb in the eye直视某人的眼睛

He couldn't look me in the eye.

47-2 她直视着他的眼睛说了不。

She looked him in the eye and said no.
