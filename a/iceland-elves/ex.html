Mind the Elves · 例句默写纸

2026-11-09 · 45 个词的例句 · 看中文写出英文例句（或填出空着的词），写完再看最后的答案。　姓名 ________　日期 ________

1mind (verb)当心、注意

1-1 出去的时候当心台阶。

Mind the step on your way out.

1-2 小心碰头，天花板很低。

Mind your head, the ceiling is low.

2take the long way round绕远路

2-1 桥封了，我们只好绕远路。

The bridge was closed, so we took the long way round.

2-2 她宁可绕远走，避开那条车多的路。

She walks the long way round to avoid the busy road.

3crew一队工作人员

3-1 六个人的队伍连夜抢修水管。

A crew of six worked through the night to fix the pipe.

3-2 摄制组一早就来布置灯光。

The film crew arrived early to set up the lights.

4on the edge of在……的边上

4-1 他们住在一片森林的边上。

They live on the edge of a forest.

4-2 这家公司已经濒临倒闭。

The company is on the edge of collapse.

5blast through（用炸药）炸开；冲破

5-1 工程师得炸开山体才能修隧道。

Engineers had to blast through the mountain to build the tunnel.

5-2 暴风雨过后，河水冲垮了那堵老墙。

The river blasted through the old wall after the storm.

6turn up（突然）出现、到场

6-1 只有五个人来开会。

Only five people turned up for the meeting.

6-2 我丢的钥匙在洗衣机里找到了。

My lost keys turned up in the washing machine.

7lore（民间）传说、口耳相传的知识

7-1 按当地的说法，那个湖深不见底。

According to local lore, the lake has no bottom.

7-2 奶奶懂很多厨房里的老窍门。

Grandma knows a lot of old kitchen lore.

8pass down传下来、传给后代

8-1 这个菜谱在我们家传了很多年。

This recipe has been passed down in our family for years.

8-2 他把对音乐的热爱传给了孩子们。

He passed down his love of music to his children.

9take offence生气、觉得被冒犯

9-1 他拿她的厨艺开玩笑，她不高兴了。

She took offence at his joke about her cooking.

9-2 别生气，我只是开玩笑。

Please don't take offence; I was only teasing.

10disturb打扰；弄乱

10-1 抱歉打扰，你的车挡住我的车了。

Sorry to disturb you, but your car is blocking mine.

10-2 鸟儿被噪音惊扰了。

The birds were disturbed by the noise.

11swear发誓、断言

11-1 我发誓我锁门了。

I swear I locked the door.

11-2 他发誓以前从没见过她。

He swore he had never seen her before.

12rule out排除（可能性）

12-1 医生已经排除了骨折的可能。

Doctors have ruled out a broken bone.

12-2 这个周末再下雨的可能不能排除。

We can't rule out more rain this weekend.

13shrug耸肩（表示不知道或无所谓）

13-1 我问她去哪儿了，他只是耸了耸肩。

When I asked where she was, he just shrugged.

13-2 她耸耸肩，笑了笑，算是回答。

She answered with a shrug and a smile.

14better safe than sorry小心总比后悔好、宁可谨慎

14-1 带件外套吧，小心总比后悔好。

Take a jacket. Better safe than sorry.

14-2 我检查了两遍烤箱，小心点没错。

I checked the oven twice; better safe than sorry.

15cautious谨慎的、小心的

15-1 雪天开车要小心。

Be cautious when you drive in the snow.

15-2 她借钱给朋友很谨慎。

She is cautious about lending money to friends.

16shape (verb)塑造、影响

16-1 小时候读的书影响了我思考的方式。

Books I read as a child shaped the way I think.

16-2 天气影响人们的穿着。

Weather shapes the way people dress.

17reroute改道、改变路线

17-1 因为马拉松，交通改道了。

Traffic was rerouted because of the marathon.

17-2 我们的航班改飞了另一个机场。

Our flight was rerouted to another airport.

18spare (verb)使免受（伤害）；饶过；抽出（时间）

18-1 大火没有烧到那座老教堂。

The fire spared the old church.

18-2 你能抽几分钟帮我一下吗？

Can you spare a few minutes to help me?

19occasionally偶尔、有时

19-1 我们偶尔周五出去吃饭。

We occasionally eat out on Fridays.

19-2 他偶尔会忘带钥匙。

He occasionally forgets his keys.

20mediator调解人

20-1 一位调解人帮双方达成了协议。

A mediator helped the two sides reach a deal.

20-2 操场上吵架时，老师常充当调解人。

Teachers often act as mediators in playground fights.

21break down（机器）出故障；（人）崩溃

21-1 我们的车在荒郊野外抛锚了。

Our car broke down in the middle of nowhere.

21-2 打印机每周一都坏。

The printer breaks down every Monday.

22fall ill生病

22-1 他考试前一天病倒了。

He fell ill the day before the exam.

22-2 很多孩子冬天会生病。

Many children fall ill in winter.

23be behind sth是……的幕后原因、在背后操纵

23-1 警方认为这几起盗窃是当地一个团伙干的。

Police think a local gang is behind the thefts.

23-2 我想知道这个恶作剧是谁干的。

I wonder who was behind the prank.

24boulder巨石、大圆石

24-1 一块巨石滚到了路上。

A huge boulder rolled onto the road.

24-2 孩子们爬上了河边的一块大石头。

The children climbed onto a boulder by the river.

25by agreement经（双方或各方）同意

25-1 经大家同意，会议改到了周五。

The meeting was moved to Friday by agreement.

25-2 双方协商一致，终止了合同。

They ended the contract by mutual agreement.

26sceptic怀疑论者、不轻信的人

26-1 连怀疑的人都对结果印象深刻。

Even the sceptics were impressed by the result.

26-2 我自己试过之前一直不信。

I was a sceptic until I tried it myself.

27roll one's eyes翻白眼（表示不屑、不耐烦）

27-1 他又讲那个笑话时，她翻了个白眼。

She rolled her eyes when he told the same joke again.

27-2 青少年常对父母翻白眼。

Teenagers often roll their eyes at their parents.

28stand-in替身、代替者

28-1 老师病了，由代课老师来上课。

The teacher was ill, so a stand-in took the class.

28-2 你不在的时候谁来替你？

Who will stand in for you while you're away?

29landscape风景、地貌

29-1 这里的地貌平坦，一片绿色。

The landscape here is flat and green.

29-2 她画山水风景。

She paints mountain landscapes.

30bulldoze用推土机推平；强行推进

30-1 老电影院被推平，给公寓楼腾地方。

The old cinema was bulldozed to make way for flats.

30-2 他们想推平公园，修个停车场。

They want to bulldoze the park and build a car park.

31put back放回原处；恢复

31-1 看完请把书放回原处。

Please put back the books when you're done.

31-2 森林一旦没了，就很难恢复。

Once a forest is gone, it's hard to put back.

32invoke援引、搬出（某种说法来支持自己）

32-1 他援引了自己保持沉默的权利。

He invoked his right to remain silent.

32-2 政客常常搬出历史来争取支持。

Politicians often invoke history to win support.

33campaign for为……开展运动、奔走呼吁

33-1 家长们奔走呼吁在学校附近修一条更安全的人行横道。

Parents campaigned for a safer crossing near the school.

33-2 她多年来一直为干净的饮用水奔走。

She has spent years campaigning for clean water.

34nature reserve自然保护区

34-1 我们在自然保护区看到了海豹。

We saw seals at the nature reserve.

34-2 这片沼泽在 1990 年成了自然保护区。

The marsh became a nature reserve in 1990.

35a second life第二次生命、重获新生

35-1 老工厂改成了艺术中心，重获新生。

The old factory got a second life as an art centre.

35-2 修理咖啡馆让坏了的烤面包机重新派上用场。

Repair cafés give broken toasters a second life.

36dwelling住处、住所（正式）

36-1 这个村子大约有两百处住房。

The village has about 200 dwellings.

36-2 考古学家在田地下发现了一处古代住所。

Archaeologists found an ancient dwelling under the field.

37play along配合、顺着（玩笑或游戏）演下去

37-1 孩子们说自己是海盗，我就顺着演了。

The kids said they were pirates, so I played along.

37-2 他不信她的话，但决定先配合着。

He didn't believe her story but decided to play along.

38partly because部分是因为

38-1 我待在家里，一部分原因是在下雨。

I stayed home, partly because it was raining.

38-2 这次旅行很便宜，部分原因是我们露营。

The trip was cheap, partly because we camped.

39bring in带来（收入）；引进

39-1 夏季节日吸引来成千上万的游客。

The summer festival brings in thousands of visitors.

39-2 她的咖啡馆赚的钱够付房租。

Her café brings in enough to pay the rent.

40whether or not不管是否……

40-1 不管下不下雨，比赛照常进行。

Whether or not it rains, the match will go ahead.

40-2 不管用不用，你都得付钱。

You have to pay whether or not you use it.

41believe in相信……存在；信任

41-1 你相信有鬼吗？

Do you believe in ghosts?

41-2 我的教练一直相信我。

My coach always believed in me.

42could do with很需要、要是有……就好了

42-1 我真想来杯茶。

I could do with a cup of tea.

42-2 这房间该好好打扫一下了。

This room could do with a good clean.

43elf (复数 elves)精灵（民间传说里的小精怪）

43-1 故事里，三个小精灵晚上帮一个穷鞋匠干活。

In the story, three elves help a poor shoemaker at night.

43-2 有些冰岛人相信学校后面那块大石头里住着一个精灵。

Some Icelanders believe an elf lives in the big rock behind the school.

44say less about A than about B与其说反映了A，不如说反映了B

44-1 他桌子乱，与其说是因为懒，不如说是因为太忙。

His messy desk says less about laziness than about how busy he is.

44-2 这部电影的成功，与其说是因为故事，不如说是因为卡司有名。

The film's success says less about the story than about its famous cast.

45Few A ..., yet surprisingly few B ...很少有人……，可出人意料的是，也很少有人……（两头都不愿表态）

45-1 很少有人喜欢考试，可出人意料的是，想取消考试的人也很少。

Few people enjoy exams, yet surprisingly few want to get rid of them.

45-2 我的朋友里很少有人说自己热爱工作，可出人意料的是，在找新工作的人也很少。

Few of my friends say they love their jobs, yet surprisingly few are looking for new ones.
