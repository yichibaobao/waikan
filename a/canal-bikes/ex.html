The details of the deal remain murky.

My headphones are always tangled.

9dripping滴着水的、湿淋淋的

Fix that dripping tap, please.

11wreckage残骸、碎片

11-1 救援人员在残骸中搜寻。

Rescuers searched the wreckage.

11-2 船的残骸被冲上了海滩。

Wreckage from the boat washed up on the beach.

12pile堆放；一堆

12-1 脏碗碟堆满了水槽。

Dirty dishes piled up in the sink.

12-2 她把书堆在桌上。

She piled the books on the table.

13move on继续前进；转到（下一件事）

13-1 我们进入下一个话题吧。

Let's move on to the next topic.

13-2 分手后，她很难走出来。

After the break-up, she found it hard to move on.

14end up最终（到某处、处于某种状态）

14-1 我们迷了路，最后到了另一个镇。

We got lost and ended up in the wrong town.

14-2 你不存钱，最后会身无分文。

If you don't save, you'll end up broke.

15a matter of debate有争议的问题

15-1 谁先发明的还有争议。

Who invented it first is a matter of debate.

15-2 它真正的年代还有一些争议。

Its real age is a matter of some debate.

16dump倾倒、随意丢弃；甩掉（恋人）

16-1 有人把一张旧沙发扔在路边。

Someone dumped an old sofa by the road.

16-2 他是被短信分手的。

He was dumped by text message.

17prankster爱恶作剧的人

17-1 一个恶作剧的人把路锥扣在了雕像头上。

A prankster put a traffic cone on the statue.

17-2 孩子们捉弄了他们的老师。

The kids played a prank on their teacher.

18a night out晚上出去玩（吃饭、喝酒、聚会）

18-1 我们为她过生日出去玩了一晚。

We had a night out for her birthday.

18-2 晚上出去玩了以后，他一觉睡到中午。

After a night out, he slept until noon.

19wreck残骸；破旧不堪的东西（或人）

19-1 他的第一辆车是辆破车。

His first car was an old wreck.

19-2 暴风雨毁了花园。

The storm wrecked the garden.

20layer层、层次

20-1 桌子上落了厚厚一层灰。

The table was covered in a thick layer of dust.

20-2 去徒步要穿得一层一层的。

Wear layers when you go hiking.

21scale规模、程度；比例

21-1 没人料到破坏的程度这么大。

Nobody expected the scale of the damage.

21-2 这个节日规模盛大。

The festival is held on a huge scale.

22battered破旧的、饱经磨损的

22-1 他还开着一辆破旧的面包车。

He still drives a battered old van.

22-2 她走到哪儿都带着一本破旧的笔记本。

She carries a battered notebook everywhere.

23precisely恰恰、正是；精确地

23-1 这正是我给你打电话的原因。

That is precisely why I called you.

23-2 火车六点整准时开出。

The train left at precisely six.

24target (n.)目标；对象

24-1 游客很容易成为小偷的目标。

Tourists are an easy target for pickpockets.

24-2 这个月我们达到了销售目标。

We hit our sales target this month.

25headache头疼；令人头疼的事

25-1 找保姆总是件头疼的事。

Finding a babysitter is always a headache.

25-2 我醒来时头疼得厉害。

I woke up with a terrible headache.

26multi-storey多层的（楼房、停车场）

26-1 我们把车停在了一个多层停车场。

We parked in a multi-storey car park.

26-2 那栋老旧的多层楼被拆了。

The old multi-storey building was pulled down.

27reputedly据说、普遍认为

27-1 据说这家餐厅的意面是全城最好吃的。

The restaurant reputedly serves the best pasta in town.

27-2 据说他是村里最有钱的人。

He is reputedly the richest man in the village.

28depot仓库；车库、停车场

28-1 公交车晚上开回总站。

The buses return to the depot at night.

28-2 丢失的自行车存放在港口附近的一个仓库里。

Lost bikes are kept at a depot near the port.

29trace back to追溯到、追查到

29-1 火灾追查到是一支蜡烛引起的。

The fire was traced back to a candle.

29-2 她的家族可以追溯到 17 世纪。

Her family can be traced back to the 1600s.

30scrap (verb)报废、当废品处理；取消

30-1 旧火车将被报废。

The old trains will be scrapped.

30-2 开了一次会，他们就放弃了这个主意。

They scrapped the idea after one meeting.

31recycle回收利用

31-1 我们的瓶子全部回收利用。

We recycle all our bottles.

31-2 这个袋子是用回收塑料做的。

This bag is made of recycled plastic.

32not so much A as B与其说是 A，不如说是 B

32-1 与其说这是工作，不如说是爱好。

It's not so much a job as a hobby.

32-2 与其说她生气，不如说她失望。

She was not so much angry as disappointed.

33delighted非常高兴的

33-1 我非常乐意帮忙。

I'd be delighted to help.

33-2 孩子们看到小狗高兴坏了。

The children were delighted with the puppy.

34spectacle景象、奇观；（引人注目的）场面

34-1 烟花真是壮观的景象。

The fireworks were a wonderful spectacle.

34-2 街头斗殴成了众人围观的场面。

The fight in the street became a public spectacle.

35routine例行公事、惯例；日常的

35-1 锻炼是我每天的固定安排。

Exercise is part of my daily routine.

35-2 那只是一次例行检查。

It was just a routine check.

36unremarkable平淡无奇的、不起眼的

36-1 这个小镇本身相当平淡无奇。

The town itself is fairly unremarkable.

36-2 他的童年平平无奇。

He had an unremarkable childhood.

37unsentimental不感情用事的、务实的

37-1 扔旧东西的时候她一点也不感情用事。

She is unsentimental about throwing old things away.

37-2 这部电影冷静地展现了战争。

The film gives an unsentimental picture of war.

38a matter of time时间问题、迟早的事

38-1 他发现只是迟早的事。

It's only a matter of time before he finds out.

38-2 成功只是时间问题。

Success is a matter of time.
