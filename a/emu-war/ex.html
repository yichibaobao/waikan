1outgunned火力不如对方；被比下去

1-1 本地队从一开始就实力不济。

The local team was outgunned from the start.

1-2 小店常常被大型连锁店压着打。

Small shops are often outgunned by big chains.

2flightless不会飞的

2-1 企鹅是不会飞的鸟。

Penguins are flightless birds.

2-2 新西兰有好几种不会飞的鸟。

New Zealand is home to several flightless species.

3-2 我们出发去找城里最好吃的披萨。

We set out to find the best pizza in town.

We need to get rid of these old boxes.

4-2 新法律旨在清除街上的垃圾。

The new law aims to rid the streets of litter.

5-1 结果那家店周一不开门。

It turned out the shop was closed on Mondays.

5-2 结果那个传言是真的。

The rumour, it turned out, was true.

Sorry, I have other plans tonight.

She met a former classmate at the station.

7-2 这栋楼以前是工厂。

The building is a former factory.

9-2 一辆出租车不知从哪儿冒了出来。

A taxi materialised out of nowhere.

10-1 很多鸟冬天往南迁徙。

Many birds migrate south for the winter.

10-2 年轻人正在迁往城市。

Young people are migrating to the cities.

11irresistible无法抗拒的、极有诱惑力的

The smell of fresh bread was irresistible.

11-2 他们给了我一个无法拒绝的条件。

They made me an irresistible offer.

12trample践踏、踩坏

12-1 牛把菜园踩得一塌糊涂。

The cows trampled the vegetable garden.

12-2 请不要踩踏花草。

Please don't trample the flowers.

13let in让……进来

13-1 别让不认识的人进来。

Don't let in anyone you don't know.

13-2 拉开窗帘让光线进来。

Open the curtains to let in the light.

14devour狼吞虎咽地吃光；如饥似渴地读

14-1 饿坏了的男孩们把整个蛋糕吃光了。

The hungry boys devoured the whole cake.

14-2 她一周就能读完一本书。

She devours a book a week.

15dispatch派遣；发送

15-1 救护车立刻被派了过去。

An ambulance was dispatched at once.

15-2 您的订单明天发货。

Your order will be dispatched tomorrow.

16ammunition弹药；（辩论的）论据

16-1 士兵们弹药打光了。

The soldiers ran out of ammunition.

16-2 他的失误给了对手新的把柄。

His mistake gave his rivals fresh ammunition.

17on paper从纸面上看、理论上

17-1 从纸面上看，他是最合适的人选。

On paper, he is the best candidate.

17-2 这笔交易纸面上看起来不错。

The deal looks good on paper.

18mismatch不相配；实力悬殊的比赛

18-1 决赛完全是一边倒。

The final was a complete mismatch.

18-2 我们挣的和花的完全对不上。

There's a mismatch between what we earn and what we spend.

19scatter（使）四散、撒

19-1 狗一跑过来，鸽子就四散飞走了。

The pigeons scattered when the dog ran up.

19-2 玩具撒得满地都是。

Toys were scattered all over the floor.

20the moment (that) ...一……就……

20-1 你一到就给我打电话。

Call me the moment you arrive.

20-2 我刚一坐下，电话就响了。

The moment I sat down, the phone rang.

21open fire开火

21-1 士兵们向目标开火。

The soldiers opened fire on the target.

21-2 我不下令就别开火。

Don't open fire until I say so.

22maddeningly令人恼火地、让人抓狂地

The internet was maddeningly slow.

22-2 说明书含糊得让人恼火。

The instructions were maddeningly vague.

23ambush伏击、埋伏

23-1 士兵们中了埋伏。

The soldiers walked into an ambush.

23-2 记者们在他家门口堵住了他。

Reporters ambushed him outside his house.

24jam（机器）卡住；堵塞

24-1 门卡住了，打不开。

The door jammed and wouldn't open.

24-2 我每次用打印机它都卡纸。

The printer jams every time I use it.

25mount安装、架设；登上

25-1 我们把电视挂到了墙上。

We mounted the TV on the wall.

25-2 门上方装了一个摄像头。

A camera was mounted above the door.

26outrun跑得比……快；超过

26-1 你跑不过猎豹。

You can't outrun a cheetah.

26-2 小偷跑得比警察还快。

The thief outran the police officer.

27bumpy颠簸的、坑坑洼洼的

27-1 翻山的路一路颠簸。

It was a bumpy ride over the mountains.

27-2 这家新店头一年开得磕磕绊绊。

The new shop had a bumpy first year.

28withdraw撤回、撤退；取（钱）

28-1 军队撤出了这座城市。

The army withdrew from the city.

She withdrew from the competition.

29mockery嘲笑、嘲弄

29-1 他的演讲在网上招来一片嘲讽。

His speech drew mockery online.

29-2 这场审判简直是对正义的嘲弄。

The trial made a mockery of justice.

30awkward尴尬的、令人难堪的；不灵活的

30-1 他讲完笑话后是一阵尴尬的沉默。

There was an awkward silence after his joke.

30-2 她问了我一个让人难堪的问题。

She asked me an awkward question.

31at sb's request应某人的要求

31-1 这首歌是应我的要求放的。

The song was played at my request.

31-2 应员工要求，会议改了时间。

The meeting was moved at the request of the staff.

32disputed有争议的、受到质疑的

32-1 结果仍有争议。

The result is still disputed.

They dispute the official figures.

33by ... estimates按……估计

33-1 据某些估计，一半的店会关门。

By some estimates, half the shops will close.

33-2 保守估计，维修要花一年。

By conservative estimates, the repairs will take a year.

34in charge负责、主管

34-1 这里谁负责？

Who's in charge here?

She is in charge of the marketing team.

35liken A to B把 A 比作 B

35-1 她把这份工作比作跑马拉松。

35-2 评论家把这部电影比作一场梦。

36pit A against B使 A 与 B 对抗

36-1 决赛让两个老对手狭路相逢。

The final pitted two old rivals against each other.

36-2 这个游戏就是和电脑斗智。

The game is about pitting your wits against the computer.

37brute force蛮力、暴力手段

37-1 他靠蛮力把罐子拧开了。

He opened the jar by brute force.

37-2 蛮力解不开这道谜题。

Brute force won't solve this puzzle.

38bounty赏金、悬赏；丰富

38-1 镇上每抓到一只老鼠就给赏金。

The town offered a bounty for every rat caught.

38-2 有人悬赏要他的人头。

There is a bounty on his head.

39sturdy结实的、坚固的

39-1 买双结实的靴子去徒步。

Buy a sturdy pair of boots for the hike.

39-2 我们需要一张更结实的桌子。

40wander闲逛、漫步；走失

40-2 别让孩子们走丢了。

41-1 据说这栋房子曾属于一位著名画家。

42-2 天气一如既往地灰蒙蒙的。
