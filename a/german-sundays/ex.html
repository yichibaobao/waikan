1hush嘘（叫人安静）；寂静

1-1 嘘，你会吵醒宝宝的！

Hush, you'll wake the baby!

1-2 人群一下子安静下来。

A hush fell over the crowd.

2efficiency效率

2-1 新系统提高了效率。

The new system has improved efficiency.

2-2 她工作效率很高。

She works with great efficiency.

3set aside留出、拨出（时间、钱）

3-1 我每天留出一小时读书。

I set aside an hour a day for reading.

3-2 他们为度假存了一笔钱。

They set aside money for the holiday.

4would not have it any other way就喜欢这样、不想要别的样子

4-1 家里三个孩子很吵，可我就喜欢这样。

Life with three kids is noisy, but I wouldn't have it any other way.

4-2 工作很辛苦，但她乐在其中。

The job is hard, but she wouldn't have it any other way.

5祈使句 + and + 结果如果……就会……

5-1 向左转，你就会看到车站。

Turn left and you'll see the station.

5-2 每天学习，你就会进步。

Study every day and you will improve.

6department store百货商店

6-1 她在百货商店买了这件外套。

She bought the coat in a department store.

6-2 很多百货商店正在关门。

Many department stores are closing down.

7high street（英式）主街、商业街

7-1 主街上新开了一家咖啡馆。

There's a new café on the high street.

7-2 网购正在冲击实体商业街。

Online shopping is hurting the high street.

8eerily怪异地、阴森地

8-1 空无一人的学校安静得诡异。

The empty school was eerily quiet.

8-2 这两个故事相似得出奇。

The two stories are eerily similar.

9strike (n.)罢工

9-1 工人们为了提高工资举行罢工。

The workers went on strike for higher pay.

9-2 铁路罢工让车站都关了。

A rail strike has closed the stations.

10restrict限制、约束

10-1 停车仅限居民。

Parking is restricted to residents.

10-2 新法律限制烟花的销售。

The new law restricts the sale of fireworks.

11constitution宪法；体质

11-1 言论自由受宪法保护。

Free speech is protected by the constitution.

11-2 她体质好，很少生病。

She has a strong constitution and rarely gets ill.

12uplift提升、振奋

12-1 音乐让每个人都很振奋。

The music gave everyone a sense of uplift.

12-2 这是一部关于友情的振奋人心的电影。

It's an uplifting film about friendship.

13exception例外

13-1 除了汤姆，大家都来了。

Everyone came, with the exception of Tom.

13-2 这次我破个例。

I'll make an exception this time.

14deprive of剥夺、使失去

14-1 囚犯被剥夺了睡眠。

The prisoners were deprived of sleep.

14-2 不应该剥夺孩子玩耍的权利。

Children should not be deprived of play.

15do a roaring trade生意兴隆

15-1 下雨天卖伞的生意火爆。

Umbrella sellers did a roaring trade in the rain.

15-2 新开的面包店生意兴隆。

The new bakery is doing a roaring trade.

16knock on敲（门、窗）

16-1 有人在敲门。

Someone is knocking on the door.

16-2 需要什么就敲我的门。

Please knock on my door if you need anything.

17extend延伸、扩展；延长

17-1 截止日期延长到了周五。

The deadline has been extended to Friday.

17-2 森林绵延好几英里。

The forest extends for miles.

18frown upon / on不赞成、对……皱眉

18-1 在这里，坐火车时吃东西会招人白眼。

Eating on the train is frowned upon here.

18-2 她父母不赞成这个主意。

Her parents frowned on the idea.

19mow割（草）

19-1 他每周六割一次草坪。

He mows the lawn every Saturday.

19-2 草该割了。

The grass needs mowing.

20drill钻（孔）；钻头

20-1 他在墙上钻了个洞。

He drilled a hole in the wall.

20-2 我们每个月有一次消防演习。

We have a fire drill every month.

21clatter哐啷声、咔嗒声

21-1 我听见厨房里碗碟碰撞的声音。

I heard the clatter of plates in the kitchen.

21-2 托盘哐啷一声掉在地上。

The tray clattered to the floor.

22the logic goes按照这种逻辑、照这个道理

22-1 照这个道理，食物越便宜，健康的选择就越难。

Cheaper food, the logic goes, means healthier choices are harder.

22-2 照这个逻辑，白天短了，活也该少了。

Shorter days, so the logic goes, should mean less work.

23have been known to（有时）会……、曾经……过

23-1 他有时会在洗澡时唱歌。

He has been known to sing in the shower.

23-2 熊曾经打开过车门。

Bears have been known to open car doors.

24spin旋转；（洗衣机）甩干

24-1 洗衣机在甩干。

The washing machine is spinning.

24-2 她转过身来看。

She spun around to look.

25retailer零售商

25-1 大型零售商降了价。

Big retailers cut their prices.

25-2 这家零售商在欧洲有 300 家店。

The retailer has 300 shops in Europe.

26loosen放松、放宽

26-1 会后他松了松领带。

He loosened his tie after the meeting.

26-2 政府打算放宽规定。

The government plans to loosen the rules.

27fierce激烈的、凶猛的

27-1 求职竞争很激烈。

There is fierce competition for jobs.

27-2 那只狗看上去很凶，其实很友好。

The dog looked fierce but was friendly.

28unlikely alliance意想不到的联盟

28-1 农民和艺术家结成了意想不到的联盟。

Farmers and artists formed an unlikely alliance.

28-2 两家对手公司加入了同一个联盟。

The two rival firms joined an alliance.

29trade union工会

29-1 她在单位加入了工会。

She joined a trade union at work.

29-2 工会号召罢工。

The unions called for a strike.

30sacred神圣的

30-1 这条河在当地人心中是神圣的。

The river is sacred to local people.

30-2 我早上的咖啡雷打不动。

My morning coffee is sacred.

31side with站在……一边、支持

31-1 我妈总是站在我姐姐那边。

My mum always sides with my sister.

31-2 法官支持了工人。

The judge sided with the workers.

32rather to one's surprise颇出乎某人意料

32-1 让我吃惊的是，她答应了。

To my surprise, she said yes.

32-2 出乎我们意料，那家店居然开着。

To our surprise, the shop was open.

33come to + 动词渐渐……、终于……

33-1 我渐渐喜欢上了一个人住。

I came to enjoy living alone.

33-2 她终于意识到他是对的。

She came to realise he was right.

34relative (n.)亲戚

34-1 这个周末我们去走亲戚。

We're visiting relatives this weekend.

34-2 她是我的一个远房亲戚。

She's a distant relative of mine.

35linger over在……上逗留、慢慢享受

35-1 我们慢悠悠地喝了一个小时咖啡。

We lingered over coffee for an hour.

35-2 考试时别在每道题上磨蹭。

Don't linger over every question in the exam.

36go quiet变安静、沉默下来

36-1 她一进来，屋里就安静了。

The room went quiet when she walked in.

36-2 晚上十点以后我的手机就静音了。

My phone goes quiet after ten at night.

37reminder提醒（的东西）

37-1 我在手机上设了个提醒。

I set a reminder on my phone.

37-2 这张照片让人想起快乐的日子。

The photo is a reminder of happy days.

38productive有成效的、高产的

38-1 这是一次很有成效的会议。

It was a very productive meeting.

38-2 我早上效率更高。

I feel more productive in the morning.

39punctual准时的、守时的

39-1 德国的火车也不总是准时。

German trains are not always punctual.

39-2 她开会总是很准时。

She is always punctual for meetings.

40as ... as ever一如既往地……

40-1 火车一如既往地晚点了。

As ever, the train was late.

40-2 她还是那么忙。

She's as busy as ever.
