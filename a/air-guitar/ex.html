Strumming Thin Air · 例句默写纸

2026-11-10 · 42 个词的例句 · 看中文写出英文例句（或填出空着的词），写完再看最后的答案。　姓名 ________　日期 ________

1strum（用手指）扫弦、弹拨

1-1 他坐在台阶上，拨着一把旧吉他。

He sat on the steps, strumming an old guitar.

1-2 她扫了几个和弦，开始唱歌。

She strummed a few chords and began to sing.

2thin air稀薄的空气；空无一物

2-1 那笔钱好像是凭空冒出来的。

The money seemed to appear out of thin air.

2-2 我的钥匙不可能凭空消失吧！

My keys can't just vanish into thin air!

3host (verb)主办、举办

3-1 巴黎在 2024 年举办了奥运会。

Paris hosted the Olympic Games in 2024.

3-2 我们学校每年春天办一次书展。

Our school hosts a book fair every spring.

4by design故意地、有意为之

4-1 这家咖啡馆小是故意的，老板想让它显得温馨。

The café is small by design; the owner wants it to feel cosy.

4-2 不管是有意还是偶然，这个计划奏效了。

Whether by design or by accident, the plan worked.

5stakes（成败的）利害、赌注

5-1 下周就期末考了，事关重大。

With the final exam next week, the stakes are high.

5-2 只是友谊赛，输赢无所谓。

It's just a friendly game, so the stakes are low.

6charm魅力、吸引力

6-1 老城有一种安静的魅力。

The old town has a quiet charm.

6-2 小小的厨房也是这套公寓的魅力之一。

The tiny kitchen is part of the flat's charm.

7roaring轰鸣的、喧闹的；（生意）兴隆的

7-1 我们坐在熊熊的炉火边喝可可。

We sat by a roaring fire and drank cocoa.

7-2 演出大获成功。

The show was a roaring success.

8leap跳、跃

8-1 猫一下跳上了桌子。

The cat leapt onto the table.

8-2 闹钟一响她就从床上蹦起来。

She leaps out of bed the moment the alarm rings.

9pull faces做鬼脸

9-1 孩子们对着镜头做鬼脸。

The kids pulled faces at the camera.

9-2 他一尝那药就皱起了脸。

He pulled a face when he tasted the medicine.

10as if + 过去完成时仿佛、好像（与事实相反）

10-1 她看着我，好像我说了什么失礼的话。

She looked at me as if I had said something rude.

10-2 他表现得好像他是老板似的。

He acts as if he were the boss.

11fixture固定节目、常设活动；固定装置

11-1 圣诞市集是这座城市冬天的固定节目。

The Christmas market is a fixture of the city's winter.

11-2 那位老人成了公园里的老面孔。

The old man became a fixture in the park.

12compete for争夺、为……而竞争

12-1 十支队伍将争夺奖杯。

Ten teams will compete for the cup.

12-2 商店在争抢越来越少的顾客。

Shops are competing for fewer customers.

13nor + 助动词 + 主语（倒装）也不……

13-1 我不喜欢咖啡，我姐姐也不喜欢。

I don't like coffee, nor does my sister.

13-2 他从没打过电话，也没写过信。

He never called, nor did he write.

14rely on依靠、依赖

14-1 很多村子靠旅游业为生。

Many villages rely on tourism.

14-2 你可以放心她会保守秘密。

You can rely on her to keep a secret.

15imaginary想象中的、虚构的

15-1 她小时候有个叫 Bo 的想象中的朋友。

As a child she had an imaginary friend called Bo.

15-2 两国之间的界线只是想象中的，并没有围栏。

The line between the two countries is imaginary; there is no fence.

16stage presence台风、舞台魅力

16-1 那位年轻歌手台风很好。

The young singer has great stage presence.

16-2 好老师也需要一点台风。

Good teachers need a bit of stage presence too.

17in other words换句话说、也就是说

17-1 他正处在两份工作之间，换句话说，他失业了。

He's between jobs; in other words, he's unemployed.

17-2 也就是说，我们需要更多时间。

In other words, we need more time.

18convince使相信、说服

18-1 她让我相信这个计划行得通。

She convinced me that the plan would work.

18-2 花了好几个小时才说服他去看医生。

It took hours to convince him to see a doctor.

19with conviction满怀信念地、坚定地

19-1 她坚定地谈起自己的计划。

She spoke with conviction about her plans.

19-2 就算弹错音，只要弹得自信也挺好听。

Even wrong notes sound fine if you play them with conviction.

20elaborate (adj.)精心制作的、复杂的

20-1 他们为婚礼做了一个精致的蛋糕。

They made an elaborate cake for the wedding.

20-2 他为迟到编了一套复杂的借口。

He gave an elaborate excuse for being late.

21dress up打扮、盛装；乔装

21-1 今晚吃饭需要穿得正式点吗？

Do I need to dress up for dinner tonight?

21-2 孩子们扮成了各种动物。

The children dressed up as animals.

22show off炫耀、卖弄

22-1 他很爱炫耀他的新车。

He loves to show off his new car.

22-2 别显摆了，好好把饭吃完。

Stop showing off and just finish your food.

23beg to differ恕难苟同、我不这么认为

23-1 你说太冷不能游泳？我可不这么觉得。

You say it's too cold to swim? I beg to differ.

23-2 影评人讨厌这部电影，观众却不这么看。

Critics hated the film, but audiences begged to differ.

24frame by frame一帧一帧地；逐步细致地

24-1 教练一帧一帧地看比赛录像。

The coach watched the race video frame by frame.

24-2 我们把这个问题一点一点地过了一遍。

We went through the problem frame by frame.

25insist坚持（说）、坚决要求

25-1 他坚称自己见过鬼。

He insists that he saw a ghost.

25-2 我妈坚持要开车送我去车站。

My mother insisted on driving me to the station.

26stamina耐力、体力

26-1 跑马拉松需要很好的耐力。

Marathon runners need a lot of stamina.

26-2 我没那个体力逛一整天街。

I don't have the stamina to shop all day.

27out of breath上气不接下气、气喘吁吁

27-1 爬完楼梯我喘得不行。

I was out of breath after climbing the stairs.

27-2 她气喘吁吁地赶到，好在没迟到。

She arrived out of breath but on time.

28wear sth on one's sleeve毫不掩饰（情感、立场）

28-1 他喜怒全写在脸上。

He wears his heart on his sleeve.

28-2 这支乐队毫不掩饰自己的政治立场。

The band wears its politics on its sleeve.

29take up开始从事（爱好、运动）；占用

29-1 她退休后开始画画。

She took up painting after she retired.

29-2 这张沙发占了半个房间。

This sofa takes up half the room.

30capture捕捉、抓住（精髓、注意力）；俘获

30-1 这张照片捕捉到了那一刻的喜悦。

The photo captures the joy of the moment.

30-2 这首歌唱出了那个夏天的氛围。

The song captured the mood of the summer.

31harmless无害的、无恶意的

31-1 大多数蜘蛛是无害的。

Most spiders are harmless.

31-2 那只是个无伤大雅的玩笑。

It was just a harmless joke.

32absurd荒唐的、荒谬可笑的

32-1 一个三明治卖那么贵，太离谱了。

It's absurd to pay that much for a sandwich.

32-2 这个主意一开始听起来很荒唐。

The idea sounded absurd at first.

33refreshing令人耳目一新的；提神的

33-1 遇到这么坦诚的人，真让人耳目一新。

It's refreshing to meet someone so honest.

33-2 一杯冰柠檬水很解渴提神。

A cold glass of lemonade is very refreshing.

34be accused of被指责、被指控（做了……）

34-1 他被指控考试作弊。

He was accused of cheating in the exam.

34-2 这家公司被指责隐瞒真相。

The company was accused of hiding the truth.

35unfair advantage不公平的优势

35-1 用计算器会让你占不公平的便宜。

Using a calculator would give you an unfair advantage.

35-2 有钱人家能给孩子买来不公平的优势。

Rich families can buy their children an unfair advantage.

36nerve胆量、勇气；神经

36-1 我没胆量约她出去。

I didn't have the nerve to ask her out.

36-2 吵闹的音乐让我心烦。

Loud music gets on my nerves.

37in principle理论上、原则上

37-1 原则上谁都可以申请。

In principle, anyone can apply.

37-2 我们原则上同意，但细节还需要再谈。

We agree in principle, but the details need work.

38in an age when在……的时代

38-1 在人人都有手机的时代，很少有人戴手表。

In an age when everyone has a phone, few people wear watches.

38-2 在消息传得飞快的时代，谣言传得更快。

In an age when news travels fast, rumours spread faster.

39rebellion反叛、叛逆；起义

39-1 把头发染成绿色是她的小小叛逆。

Dyeing her hair green was her small rebellion.

39-2 国王镇压了起义。

The king crushed the rebellion.

40sore疼痛的、酸痛的

40-1 昨天跑完步腿好酸。

My legs are sore after yesterday's run.

40-2 我嗓子疼，就待在家吧。

I've got a sore throat, so I'll stay home.

41judging by从……来看、根据……判断

41-1 看这排队的阵势，菜一定好吃。

Judging by the queue, the food must be good.

41-2 看她笑得这么开心，应该是考过了。

Judging by her smile, she passed the test.

42anything but绝不、根本不

42-1 这次考试一点也不简单。

The exam was anything but easy.

42-2 他看起来一点也不开心。

He looked anything but happy.
