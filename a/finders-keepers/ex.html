Finders, Keepers · 例句默写纸

2026-10-31 · 39 个词的例句 · 看中文写出英文例句（或填出空着的词），写完再看最后的答案。　姓名 ________　日期 ________

1slump onto瘫坐在……上

1-1 上完夜班，他一屁股瘫坐在沙发上，立刻睡着了。

After the night shift, he slumped onto the sofa and fell asleep at once.

1-2 筋疲力尽的徒步者们瘫坐在山顶的草地上。

The exhausted hikers slumped onto the grass at the top of the hill.

2doze off打起瞌睡

2-1 那堂课太无聊，我一直在打瞌睡。

I kept dozing off during the boring lecture.

2-2 爷爷又在电视机前打起了瞌睡。

Grandpa dozed off in front of the TV again.

3stuffed with塞满了……

3-1 行李箱里塞满了给家人的礼物。

The suitcase was stuffed with gifts for her family.

3-2 他的收件箱里塞满了没读的邮件。

His inbox is stuffed with unread emails.

4improbable不太可能的，出人意料的

4-1 有人会无视这样的提议，这似乎不太可能。

It seems improbable that anyone would ignore such an offer.

4-2 两个朋友在最出人意料的地方重逢了：冰岛的一个小机场。

The two friends met again in the most improbable place: a tiny airport in Iceland.

5come forward站出来（认领、作证）

5-1 警方呼吁目击者站出来。

Police have asked witnesses to come forward.

5-2 没有人出面来认领那个丢失的包。

Nobody came forward to claim the lost bag.

6staggering惊人的

6-1 这个项目的成本高得惊人。

The cost of the project is staggering.

6-2 她以惊人的优势赢得了比赛。

She won the race by a staggering margin.

7by sheer volume单凭数量

7-1 这家公司单凭数量就压倒了对手，出货量是对方的十倍。

The company drowned its rivals by sheer volume, shipping ten times more products.

7-2 单从数量上看，英语在学术出版中占据主导。

By sheer volume, English dominates academic publishing.

8lead the pack遥遥领先

8-1 在准点率方面，日本的新干线遥遥领先。

Japan's bullet trains lead the pack on punctuality.

8-2 她的初创公司已经开始在清洁能源领域领跑。

Her start-up has begun to lead the pack in clean energy.

9X is another storyX 则另当别论

9-1 煮意面很简单，从头自己做就是另一回事了。

Cooking pasta is easy. Making it from scratch is another story.

9-2 他钢琴弹得不错，唱歌就另当别论了。

He plays the piano well, but singing is another story.

10entitle sb. to sth.赋予某人……的权利

10-1 有效门票可以让你免费领取一杯饮料。

A valid ticket entitles you to a free drink.

10-2 员工有权享受四周带薪假期。

Employees are entitled to four weeks of paid leave.

11waive放弃（权利、费用）

11-1 银行同意免除滞纳金。

The bank agreed to waive the late fee.

11-2 学生如果能出示低收入证明，可以免交这项费用。

Students may waive the fee if they show proof of low income.

12infrastructure基础设施；这里引申为配套机制

12-1 这个国家正在大力投资交通基础设施。

The country is investing heavily in transport infrastructure.

12-2 没有配套机制的支撑，再好的点子也会落空。

Good ideas fail without the infrastructure to support them.

13gloriously dull乏味得恰到好处（反语式夸赞）

13-1 那场会议乏味得恰到好处：没出任何岔子，也什么都没发生。

The meeting was gloriously dull: nothing went wrong, and nothing happened.

13-2 经历了混乱的一年，他渴望在家过一个平平淡淡的周末。

After a chaotic year, he longed for a gloriously dull weekend at home.

14a lesson that travels可以推广到别处的经验

14-1 东京的失物招领体系是一个可以推广的经验：任何城市都能照搬。

Tokyo's lost-property system is a lesson that travels: any city could copy it.

14-2 她的教学方法是一条可以推广到各个学科的经验。

Her approach to teaching is a lesson that travels across subjects.

15counterintuitively违反直觉地

15-1 违反直觉的是，休息得更多的学生分数反而更高。

Counterintuitively, students who rested more scored higher.

15-2 违反直觉的是，人少的团队往往完成项目更快。

Counterintuitively, a smaller team often finishes the project faster.

16exhortation劝诫，规劝

16-1 教练再怎么规劝也提振不了队伍的士气。

No exhortation from the coach could lift the team's spirits.

16-2 这场演讲是一番冗长的规劝，要大家更加努力。

The speech was a long exhortation to work harder.

17legislate立法规定；这里指靠法律管控

17-1 礼貌是没法靠立法规定出来的。

You cannot legislate good manners.

17-2 议会打算立法禁止虚假评价。

Parliament plans to legislate against fake reviews.

18curse (cursing)咒骂，骂脏话；懊恼地发泄几句

18-1 他踢到了脚趾，低声咒骂起来。

He stubbed his toe and began cursing under his breath.

18-2 伞被风吹翻的时候，她咒骂起这场雨。

She cursed the rain when her umbrella blew inside out.

19log (logged)登记，记录在案

19-1 每一条投诉都会登记在系统里。

Every complaint is logged in the system.

19-2 护士每小时记录一次病人的体温。

The nurse logged the patient's temperature every hour.

20oblige sb. to do sth.责成某人做某事；（法律上）要求某人必须……

20-1 法律要求雇主提供安全的工作场所。

The law obliges employers to provide a safe workplace.

20-2 司机必须在红灯前停车。

Drivers are obliged to stop at a red light.

21attendant服务人员；station attendant 是站务员

21-1 一位站务员帮她找到了正确的站台。

A station attendant helped her find the right platform.

21-2 空乘人员给我们递上了茶。

The flight attendant offered us tea.

22payable应付的，须支付的

22-1 这笔费用须在 30 天内支付。

The fee is payable within 30 days.

22-2 支票请开成以学校为收款人。

Cheques should be made payable to the university.

23spectacularadj. 壮观的；惊人的；（情况）更离谱的

23-1 东京湾上空的烟花美不胜收。

The fireworks over Tokyo Bay were spectacular.

23-2 这家初创公司的增长惊人，垮台也同样惊人。

The start-up's growth was spectacular; so was its collapse.

24Finders, keepers谁捡到归谁

24-1 我在公交车上捡到一张十英镑的钞票，我弟弟咧嘴一笑：“谁捡到归谁！”

I found a ten-pound note on the bus, and my brother grinned: “Finders, keepers!”

24-2 孩子们在操场上念着“捡到归己，丢了活该”，但法律就没这么宽容了。

Children chant “finders keepers, losers weepers” in the playground, but the law is less forgiving.

25civicadj. 市民的；公民的；城市的

25-1 很多人把投票看作公民的义务。

Many people see voting as a civic duty.

25-2 市政厅是这座城市公共生活的中心。

The town hall is the centre of civic life.

26largely (= mostly)adv. 基本上；主要地；大体上

26-1 这份报告基本上被媒体忽视了。

The report was largely ignored by the press.

26-2 这次延误主要是因为天气不好。

The delay was largely due to bad weather.

27one stop too late晚了一站（睡过站）

27-1 我在山手线上睡着了，醒来时已经坐过了一站。

I fell asleep on the Yamanote Line and woke up one stop too late.

27-2 她听到广播时已经过了一站，只好折返。

She noticed the announcement one stop too late and had to double back.

28minusprep. 少了……；没有了……（口语化）

28-1 他度假回来了，行李却丢了。

He came back from holiday minus his luggage.

28-2 她走了进来，脸上少了往常的笑容。

She walked in, minus her usual smile.

29by the time ...等到……的时候（已经……）

29-1 我赶到车站时，末班车已经开走了。

By the time I reached the station, the last train had gone.

29-2 等你读到这条消息的时候，我应该已经降落在上海了。

By the time you read this, I will have landed in Shanghai.

30possessionn. 个人财产；所有物

30-1 她最珍视的物品是祖父留下的一台旧相机。

Her most treasured possession is an old camera from her grandfather.

30-2 请乘客随时随身携带好个人物品。

Passengers are asked to keep their possessions with them at all times.

31... and all连同……一起；连……也（口语）

31-1 她把三明治整个吞了下去，连面包边也不剩。

She swallowed the sandwich, crust and all.

31-2 他掉进了河里，连手机也一起掉了进去。

He fell into the river, phone and all.

32hand in上交；提交（作业、失物、辞呈等）

32-1 请在周五之前交上论文。

Please hand in your essays by Friday.

32-2 她把捡到的钱包交给了最近的警亭。

She handed in the lost wallet at the nearest police box.

33in practice (↔ in theory)事实上；实际上（对比：in theory 理论上）

33-1 理论上这个系统很简单，实际操作起来却要花好几个小时。

In theory the system is simple; in practice, it takes hours.

33-2 这条规定对所有人适用，但实际上很少被执行。

The rule applies to everyone, but in practice it is rarely enforced.

34reportedlyadv. 据报道；据说

34-1 据报道，这位歌手已经取消了巡演。

The singer has reportedly cancelled her tour.

34-2 据说这家公司去年亏损了数百万。

The company reportedly lost millions last year.

35lie in ...在于……（原因、关键所在）

35-1 她成功的秘诀在于努力，而不是天赋。

The secret of her success lies in hard work, not talent.

35-2 问题出在数据的收集方式上。

The problem lies in how the data is collected.

36not merely不仅仅（是）

36-1 这座博物馆不仅仅是一栋建筑，更是一座活的档案馆。

The museum is not merely a building but a living archive.

36-2 这不仅仅是钱的问题。

It is not merely a matter of money.

37virtuen. 美德；优点

37-1 耐心是一种美德。

Patience is a virtue.

37-2 这个方案最大的优点就是简单。

The plan's main virtue is its simplicity.

38no amount of ... will ...再多的……也不会……

38-1 再多的道歉也无法挽回这些损失。

No amount of apologies will undo the damage.

38-2 再多的咖啡也不会让我爱上周一的早晨。

No amount of coffee will make me enjoy Monday mornings.

39from scratch从零开始；从头做起

39-1 她的文件全丢了，只好从头重写报告。

She lost all her files and had to write the report from scratch.

39-2 这个蛋糕是用原材料自己做的，不是用预拌粉。

This cake is made from scratch, not from a box mix.
