Nothing Happens, Beautifully · 例句默写纸

2026-11-13 · 40 个词的例句 · 看中文写出英文例句（或填出空着的词），写完再看最后的答案。　姓名 ________　日期 ________

1turn A into B把 A 变成 B

1-1 她把车库改成了一家小咖啡馆。

She turned her garage into a small café.

1-2 一夜之间雨变成了雪。

The rain turned into snow overnight.

2boredom无聊、厌倦

2-1 他因为无聊开始画画。

He started drawing out of boredom.

2-2 长途飞行是对无聊的考验。

Long flights are a test of boredom.

3draw (an audience)吸引（观众、人群）

3-1 这个节日每年吸引成千上万的游客。

The festival draws thousands of visitors every year.

3-2 街头歌手很快就吸引了一群人。

The street singer soon drew a crowd.

4plot（故事的）情节

4-1 这部电影画面很棒，可情节很弱。

The film looks great, but the plot is weak.

4-2 别给我剧透情节！

Don't tell me the plot!

5whatsoever（用于否定）丝毫、一点也

5-1 毫无疑问。

There is no doubt whatsoever.

5-2 他对足球一点兴趣都没有。

He had no interest whatsoever in football.

6hungry for渴望、渴求

6-1 这支球队渴望一场胜利。

The team is hungry for a win.

6-2 年轻人渴望新想法。

Young people are hungry for new ideas.

7assume假定、以为

7-1 我还以为你已经吃过了。

I assumed you'd already eaten.

7-2 永远别以为公交会准时。

Never assume the bus will be on time.

8broadcaster广播电视机构；播音员

8-1 BBC 是英国最有名的广播电视机构。

The BBC is Britain's best-known broadcaster.

8-2 她是一名体育节目主持人。

She works as a sports broadcaster.

9in full完整地、全部地

9-1 请写全名。

Please write your name in full.

9-2 账单须在周五前全额付清。

The bill must be paid in full by Friday.

10to speak of值得一提的

10-1 这个村子几乎没什么商店。

The village has no shops to speak of.

10-2 上个月几乎没下雨。

We had no rain to speak of last month.

11tune in收看、收听（节目）

11-1 数百万人收看了决赛。

Millions tuned in to watch the final.

11-2 下周继续收看。

Tune in next week for more.

12at some point在某个时候

12-1 我不知什么时候把钥匙弄丢了。

At some point I lost my keys.

12-2 每个人都会在某个时候感到孤独。

Everyone feels lonely at some point.

13without a break不间断地、不休息地

13-1 他连续工作了十个小时没休息。

He worked for ten hours without a break.

13-2 雨连续下了三天。

The rain fell for three days without a break.

14jagged参差不齐的、锯齿状的

14-1 小心，这玻璃边缘很锋利。

Be careful, the glass has jagged edges.

14-2 嶙峋的群山耸立在湖的上方。

Jagged mountains rose above the lake.

15gather聚集、集合；收集

15-1 一群人聚过来看烟花。

A crowd gathered to watch the fireworks.

15-2 我们捡柴准备生火。

We gathered wood for the fire.

16Then came + 主语（倒装）接着出现了……

16-1 先是打雷，接着下起了雨。

First there was thunder; then came the rain.

16-2 公交车来了！

Here comes the bus!

17hearth壁炉炉床；家

17-1 一只猫睡在壁炉边。

A cat slept by the hearth.

17-2 他们围坐在炉火边讲故事。

They sat around the hearth telling stories.

18half-seriously半开玩笑地、半认真地

18-1 他半开玩笑地提议我们搬到海边去。

He suggested, half-seriously, that we move to the beach.

18-2 她半开玩笑地怪罪那只猫。

She half-seriously blamed the cat.

19stack堆放、码放；一摞

19-1 会后请把椅子摞起来。

Please stack the chairs after the meeting.

19-2 书一摞摞堆在地上。

Books were stacked on the floor.

20wear thin（新鲜感、耐心）渐渐消失；让人腻

20-1 新鲜感很快就没了。

The novelty soon wore thin.

20-2 我的耐心快用完了。

My patience is wearing thin.

21be proved wrong被证明是错的

21-1 专家们被证明错了。

The experts were proved wrong.

21-2 她努力证明他们看错了。

She worked hard to prove them wrong.

22come to be known as后来被称为

22-1 这一带后来被称为小意大利。

The area came to be known as Little Italy.

22-2 她后来被称为爵士女王。

She came to be known as the queen of jazz.

23appeal (n.)吸引力、魅力

23-1 我不懂高尔夫有什么吸引力。

I don't understand the appeal of golf.

23-2 这个想法很受欢迎。

The idea has wide appeal.

24glance at瞥一眼、扫一眼

24-1 她瞥了一眼手机，笑了。

She glanced at her phone and smiled.

24-2 他连菜单都没看一眼。

He didn't even glance at the menu.

25permission许可、准许

25-1 在这里拍摄需要许可。

You need permission to film here.

25-2 他未经允许就把车开走了。

He took the car without permission.

26slow down放慢、慢下来

26-1 慢点，你开太快了！

Slow down, you're driving too fast!

26-2 退休后他终于放慢了节奏。

After retiring, he finally slowed down.

27grab attention抓住注意力、吸引眼球

27-1 鲜艳的颜色很吸引眼球。

Bright colours grab attention.

27-2 这个标题就是为了吸引你的注意。

The headline is meant to grab your attention.

28hold on to紧紧抓住；保留

28-1 抓紧扶手。

Hold on to the handrail.

28-2 她把那封信保留了很多年。

She held on to the letter for years.

29cliffhanger悬念结尾

29-1 这一集在悬念处戛然而止。

The episode ended on a cliffhanger.

29-2 我讨厌留悬念的结尾！

I hate cliffhangers!

30ask nothing of对……毫无要求

30-1 好朋友对你别无所求。

Good friends ask nothing of you.

30-2 这份工作对你要求很高。

This job will ask a lot of you.

31reward A with B用 B 回报 A

31-1 爬上去就能看到绝美的风景。

The climb rewards you with amazing views.

31-2 她的努力得到了回报。

She was rewarded for her hard work.

32patience耐心

32-1 学语言需要耐心。

Learning a language takes patience.

32-2 感谢您的耐心等待。

Thank you for your patience.

33restorative能恢复体力（精神）的

33-1 一杯茶可以让人恢复不少精神。

A cup of tea can be very restorative.

33-2 他们在海边度过了休养身心的一周。

They spent a restorative week by the sea.

34much like很像、和……差不多

34-1 和她妈妈一样，她爱唱歌。

Much like her mother, she loves to sing.

34-2 这座城市看起来和五十年前差不多。

The city looks much like it did fifty years ago.

35at work在起作用；在工作

35-1 那天是运气在起作用。

Luck was at work that day.

35-2 他还在上班。

He's still at work.

36admire欣赏；钦佩

36-1 我们停下来欣赏日落。

We stopped to admire the sunset.

36-2 我钦佩她的勇气。

I admire her courage.

37hard to fathom难以理解

37-1 他的决定令人费解。

His decision was hard to fathom.

37-2 我搞不懂她为什么走。

I can't fathom why she left.

38speak for itself / themselves不言自明、说明一切

38-1 数字说明了一切。

The numbers speak for themselves.

38-2 她的作品本身就说明了一切。

Her work speaks for itself.

39oddly奇怪地、出奇地

39-1 奇怪的是，没有人注意到。

Oddly, nobody noticed.

39-2 房间里安静得出奇。

The room was oddly quiet.

40gripping扣人心弦的、引人入胜的

40-1 这是个扣人心弦的侦探故事。

It's a gripping detective story.

40-2 比赛直到最后一分钟都很紧张刺激。

The match was gripping until the last minute.
